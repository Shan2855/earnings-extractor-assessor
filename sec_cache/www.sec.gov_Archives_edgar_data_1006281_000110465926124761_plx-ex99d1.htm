Protalix BioTherapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Business Results

Company to host conference call and webcast today at 8:00 a.m. ET

●Total revenues increased by $32.0 million year to date to $75.6 million, from $43.6 million for the same period in 2025
●Net income was $4.0 million for the third quarter, up from $2.4 million in the third quarter of 2025, and net income was $26.1 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026
●Management expects to meet or exceed 2026 guidance for total revenues of approximately $78.0 million to $83.0 million
●The PRX-115 Phase 2 RELEASE study remains on track, aiming to improve adherence and durability of response in uncontrolled gout, a substantial unmet need; top-line results anticipated in the second half of 2027
●Cash, cash equivalents, and short-term bank deposits were $48.5 million as of September 30, 2026

CARMIEL, Israel, November 10, 2026 -- Protalix BioTherapeutics, Inc. (NYSE American:PLX), a biopharmaceutical company focused on the discovery, development, production, and commercialization of innovative therapeutics for rare diseases with significant unmet needs, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business and clinical update.

“We concluded the first nine months of 2026 with revenues of $75.6 million, reflecting our continued progress and growing financial strength which supports our ability to execute our strategy and advance our key priorities,” said Dror Bashan, President and Chief Executive Officer of Protalix BioTherapeutics. “We end the quarter with cash and deposits of $48.5 million, a position of strength that enables us to continue advancing PRX-115 for uncontrolled gout. Uncontrolled gout is a disease with low quality of life and high unmet need and represents a blockbuster uricase market opportunity. Current uricase therapy has challenges including infusion burden, immunogenicity with loss of efficacy, and safety concerns. We designed PRX-115 for reduced immunogenicity and less frequent dosing that could reduce infusion burden and improve patient adherence. We look forward to top-line results from the RELEASE study in the second half of 2027.”

About Uncontrolled Gout

Gout is a metabolic disorder characterized by elevated blood uric acid that causes recurrent inflammatory arthritis and joint damage. An estimated 11.3 million people in the U.S. have gout.1 Rheumatologists report that approximately 25% of their patients have blood uric acid levels above target, which can lead to uncontrolled gout.2 An estimated 180,000 patients in the U.S. have uncontrolled gout.2 Uncontrolled gout is a severe disease with high morbidity, severe pain, and low quality of life.

​

About PRX-115

PRX-115 is a recombinant PEGylated uricase in development for uncontrolled gout, produced using Protalix’s ProCellEx® plant cell-based protein expression platform. Current uricase therapy has challenges including infusion burden, immunogenicity with loss of efficacy, and safety concerns. PRX-115 is designed for high specific activity, prolonged circulating half-life, and reduced immunogenicity.3 In the Phase 1 study of adults with elevated plasma uric acid levels, single infusions of PRX-115 lowered plasma uric acid levels rapidly and were generally well tolerated. At 36 mg, mean plasma uric acid stayed below the 6.0 mg/dL target for up to 12 weeks after a single infusion. Protalix believes PRX-115 has best-in-class potential for uncontrolled gout.

​

About the Phase 2 RELEASE Study (PRX-115)

RELEASE (NCT07280156) is a Phase 2, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of PRX-115 in patients with uncontrolled gout. The study tests a fixed 36 mg dose in five treatment arms of 30 patients each: PRX-115 every 4 weeks without methotrexate, PRX-115 every 4, 6, or 8 weeks with methotrexate, and placebo. The primary endpoint is the proportion of patients who demonstrated a reduction in serum uric acid levels to below 6.0 mg/dL for at least 80% of the time during Month 6. Top-line results are expected in the second half of 2027.

About Protalix BioTherapeutics, Inc.

Protalix is a biopharmaceutical company focused on the discovery, development, production, and commercialization of innovative therapeutics for rare diseases. Protalix has researched, developed, and currently manufactures two enzyme replacement therapies that are currently available in multiple markets. These therapies are recombinant therapeutic proteins expressed through Protalix’s proprietary plant cell-based expression system, ProCellEx®. ProCellEx is a unique plant cell-based system that enables Protalix to produce recombinant proteins in an industrial-scale manner with no exposure to mammalian cells. Protalix is the first company to gain U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) approval of a protein produced through plant cell-based in suspension expression system. Protalix has licensed to Pfizer Inc. the worldwide development and commercialization rights to taliglucerase alfa, Elelyso®, for the treatment of Gaucher disease, excluding in Brazil where Protalix retains full rights.
