EXHIBIT 99.1


SolarMax Technology Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

RIVERSIDE, CA – November 16, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – SolarMax Technology, Inc. (Nasdaq SMXT) (“SolarMax” or the “Company”), an integrated solar energy company, reported financial results for the three months and the nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

 

 

•

Revenue: $6.7 million, down 29% from $30.6 million in the third quarter of 2025.

 

 

•

Gross profit: $699,000, down 27% from $956,000 in the third quarter of 2025.

 

 

•

Total operating expense: $4.8 million, compared with $3.1 million in the third quarter of 2025.

 

 

•

Net loss: $4.2 million, or $0.88 per share, compared with a net loss of $2.3 million, or $0.51 per share in the third quarter of 2025.

 

“Despite a challenging operating environment, our solar and LED businesses continued to demonstrate resilience, with revenue excluding large-scale EPC projects increasing 2% in the third quarter and 27% for the first nine months of 2026 compared to the respective prior-year periods,” stated David Hsu, CEO of SolarMax. “Looking ahead, we are encouraged by the potential for a more favorable environment for renewable energy investment and development. With our broad capabilities, we believe SolarMax is positioned to capitalize on emerging opportunities as market conditions improve.”

 

About SolarMax Technology Inc.

 

SolarMax, based in California and founded in 2008, is a leader within the solar and renewable energy sector focused on making sustainable energy both accessible and affordable. SolarMax has established a strong presence in southern California and, commencing in the third quarter of 2025, expanded its operations to include EPC services for EPC projects. SolarMax is looking to generate growth with strategic initiatives that aim to scale commercial solar development services and provide EPC services for industrial projects and LED lighting solutions in the US while expanding its residential solar operations. For more information, visit www.solarmaxtech.com.
