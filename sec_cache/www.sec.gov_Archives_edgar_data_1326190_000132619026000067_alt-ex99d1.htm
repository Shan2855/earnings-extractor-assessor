Altimmune Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Business Update

​

Global PERFORMA Phase 3 trial in MASH progressing as planned

Positive RECLAIM Phase 2 data in AUD highlighted in a late-breaking oral presentation

at AASLD The Liver Meeting® 2026

End-of-Phase 2 meeting with FDA for AUD program scheduled for December 2026

$480 million in cash, cash equivalents and investments as of September 30, 2026

Webcast to be held today at 8:30 a.m. ET

​

MORRISTOWN, NJ – November 12, 2026 – Altimmune, Inc. (Nasdaq: ALT), a biopharmaceutical company advancing pemvidutide, an investigational balanced 1:1 glucagon/GLP-1 dual receptor agonist, in serious liver diseases, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a corporate update.

“We continue to make significant progress across all our clinical development programs, as we execute our strategy of building a liver franchise around pemvidutide” said Jerry Durso, Chief Executive Officer and Chairman of the Board of Altimmune. “Our global PERFORMA Phase 3 trial in MASH is ramping up, and we are very encouraged by the progress to date and the enthusiasm we see from the MASH community. In AUD, we look forward to engaging with the FDA in an End-of-Phase 2 meeting where we will leverage our strong RECLAIM data to discuss a Phase 3 registrational program. Following the recent AASLD meeting, our conviction in pemvidutide’s potential is stronger than ever, as we focus on delivering meaningful benefit to patients with serious liver diseases.”

​

Key Highlights and Anticipated Milestones

​

Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated Steatohepatitis (MASH)

●Pemvidutide was granted Breakthrough Therapy Designation by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) based on 24-week data from the IMPACT Phase 2b trial
●IMPACT Phase 2b 48-week data published in The Lancet Gastroenterology & Hepatology in a paper titled “Safety and efficacy of weekly pemvidutide versus placebo for metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (IMPACT): 48-week results from a multicentre, randomised, double-blind, phase 2b study”
●The global PERFORMA Phase 3 MASH trial is progressing as planned
●The PERFORMA 52-week topline data readout is anticipated in 2029

​

Alcohol Use Disorder (AUD)

●Pemvidutide was granted Fast Track designation by the FDA for the treatment of AUD
●An End-of-Phase 2 meeting with the FDA is scheduled for December 2026 to discuss the Phase 3 registrational program in AUD

​

Alcohol-associated Liver Disease (ALD)

●Enrollment is complete in the RESTORE Phase 2 trial in ALD, with topline data expected in the second half of 2027

​

Scientific Data on Pemvidutide in MASH and AUD Presented at AASLD The Liver Meeting® 2026

●A late-breaking oral presentation: Efficacy and Safety of Pemvidutide in the Treatment of Adults with Alcohol Use Disorder: Results from RECLAIM, a 24-Week Phase 2 Randomized Double-Blind Placebo Controlled Trial on the RECLAIM Phase 2 data in AUD was delivered by Dr. Henry R. Kranzler, University of Pennsylvania Perelman School of Medicine
●An oral presentation: Pemvidutide Treatment Results in Concurrent Reductions in Multiple Non-Invasive Tests for Hepatic Inflammation and Fibrosis: 48-Week Responder Analyses of the IMPACT Phase 2b Trial in Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated Steatohepatitis was delivered by Dr. Naim Alkhouri, Summit Clinical Research
●A poster presentation: Gastrointestinal Tolerability of Pemvidutide Without Dose Titration in Patients with Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated Steatohepatitis from the Phase 2b IMPACT Trial
●An oral presentation: Pemvidutide Treatment Reduces Alcohol Intake in a Lean Golden Syrian Hamster Model

Financial Results for the Three Months Ended September 30, 2026

●Altimmune reported cash, cash equivalents and investments totaling $480 million as of September 30, 2026
●Research and development (R&D) expenses were $27.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $15.0 million in the same period in 2025, with the increase driven primarily by the ongoing PERFORMA Phase 3 trial in MASH and RESTORE Phase 2 trial in ALD. R&D expenses for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, included $20.3 million in direct costs related to pemvidutide development activities
●General and administrative (G&A) expenses were $8.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $5.9 million in the same period in 2025. The increase was driven primarily by an increase in compensation expenses and professional services
●Interest income was $4.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026
●Net loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was $31.6 million, or $0.15 net loss per share, compared to a net loss of $19.0 million, or $0.21 net loss per share, in the same period in 2025

About Pemvidutide

Pemvidutide is a novel, investigational peptide with balanced 1:1 glucagon/GLP-1 dual receptor agonist activity that has an effect on reducing liver fat, inflammation, and fibrosis, in development for the treatment of metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH), alcohol use disorder (AUD) and alcohol-associated liver disease (ALD). The activation of glucagon receptors results in direct effects on the liver, while GLP-1 receptors mediate metabolic effects such as appetite suppression and weight loss and are involved in pathways related to craving and reward.

​

The FDA granted Fast Track designations to pemvidutide for the treatment of MASH and AUD, as well as Breakthrough Therapy Designation for MASH. In December 2025, the Company announced topline 48-week data from the IMPACT Phase 2b trial in MASH. In July 2026, the PERFORMA Phase 3 trial, a global, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group study of pemvidutide in patients with MASH, was initiated. In July 2026, the Company announced positive topline results from the RECLAIM Phase 2 trial in AUD. In August 2026, the Company announced completion of enrollment of the RESTORE Phase 2 trial in ALD.

​

About Altimmune

Altimmune is a biopharmaceutical company developing pemvidutide for patients with serious liver diseases. Pemvidutide, a unique dual-action investigational therapy targeting both glucagon and GLP-1 receptors in a balanced 1:1 ratio, is in late-stage clinical development for the treatment of metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH), recently showed robust results in the RECLAIM Phase 2 trial in alcohol use disorder (AUD) and is being studied in alcohol-associated liver disease (ALD). For more information, please visit www.altimmune.com.
