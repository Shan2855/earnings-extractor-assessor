Light & Wonder, Inc.
Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Light & Wonder Delivered Another Quarter of Earnings Growth and Strong Cash Generation, Driven by Its Content-Led, Cross-Platform Business Model
Continued Scaling of High-Quality Recurring Revenue(1) with North American Gaming Operations Premium Installed Base Growing for a 25th Consecutive Quarter
Robust Product and Content Roadmap Exhibited at Australasian Gaming Expo (“AGE”) and Global Gaming Expo (“G2E”) Expected to Build Momentum into Q4 FY 2026 and FY 2027
Financial Highlights
•Net income increased 17% to $133 million, up 28% on a per share basis(2), alongside Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA (“Consolidated AEBITDA”)(3) growth of 2%, Adjusted NPATA(3) growth of 7%, and EPSa(2)(3) growth of 17%.
•Continued execution on our strategy to scale high-quality recurring revenue(1), leading to enhanced cash flow, driven by a 2,657-unit(4) increase in North American Gaming operations premium installed base and the addition of 1,487 Grover charitable gaming (“Grover”) units added on a year-over-year basis, while iGaming achieved another quarter of double-digit year-over-year growth on strong 1PP content(5).
•Generated another quarter of strong cash flow, with net cash provided by operating activities up 28% and Adjusted free cash flow(3) up 21%. Segment AEBITDA margins (“margin”)(6) remained strong, reflecting our focus on operational efficiency, product mix and cash management.
•Balance sheet deleveraged, with the principal face value of debt outstanding(7) reduced by $153 million during the quarter to $5.0 billion, from $5.2 billion as of June 30, 2026, and net debt leverage ratio(3) reduced to 3.3x, from 3.4x as of June 30, 2026. The Company remains on track to continue to reduce net debt leverage ratio(3) toward 3.0x by the end of FY 2026 and below 3.0x during 1H FY 2027(8).

LAS VEGAS — November 4, 2026 — Light & Wonder, Inc. (ASX: LNW) (“Light & Wonder,” “L&W,” “we” or the “Company”) today reported results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Light & Wonder delivered another quarter of earnings growth and strong margins, underpinned by our content-led and cross-platform business model and continued strong cash flow generation. At G2E, we showcased our robust product and content roadmap, which is expected to drive momentum in the fourth quarter of 2026 and beyond.
Third quarter consolidated revenue was $824 million, down 2% year-over-year, and year-to-date revenue grew 1% year-over-year to $2,440 million. We continue to improve our quality of earnings by focusing on growing recurring revenue(1). Gaming operations (including Grover) and iGaming revenues were the primary growth drivers, both supported by operational momentum and content strength, partially offset by softness in Gaming systems and SciPlay revenue.

Net income was $133 million or $1.72 per share(1) for the quarter, up 17% and 28% year-over-year, respectively. Net cash provided by operating activities was $236 million, a 28% increase as compared to the prior year period.
The third quarter demonstrated continued earnings discipline and strong margins. Consolidated AEBITDA(2) grew 2% to $383 million, while Adjusted NPATA(2) increased 7% to $163 million, or 17% growth on a per share basis (“EPSa”)(1)(2) to $2.12, as compared to the prior year period. Adjusted free cash flow(2) was $181 million, a 21% increase year-over-year, underscoring the continued strength of our cash conversion.
Gaming revenue decreased 1% year-over-year to $554 million, from $558 million in the prior year period, due to declines in Gaming systems and Table products revenue. These decreases were partially offset by a 5% increase in Gaming machine sales revenue to $199 million and a 3% increase in Gaming operations revenue to $248 million, reflecting installed base growth and higher average daily revenue per unit.
North American Gaming operations premium installed base extended its growth streak to a 25th consecutive quarter, adding 2,657 units year-over-year, while Grover expanded by 1,487 units on a year-over-year basis. Gaming machine sales totaled 8,906 new units shipped globally in the quarter, at the high end of our prior guidance range, including more than 5,800 new units in North America.
iGaming continued to demonstrate strong execution, delivering another quarter of double-digit revenue growth of 13%. This was driven by continuing momentum in North America, 1PP content proliferation and partner network growth.
SciPlay revenue continued to reflect ongoing softness in the broader social casino free-to-play market. Direct-to-consumer (“DTC”) continues to grow as a percent of revenue, supporting earnings.
Matt Wilson, President and Chief Executive Officer of Light & Wonder, said, “Our content-centric operating model is enhancing our recurring revenue(3) business, as evidenced in the results. Sustained investment in studios and content is driving strong franchise performance across the portfolio, and our roadmap was well received at both the AGE and G2E, which builds our confidence heading into the fourth quarter and 2027. Gaming momentum continues, evidenced by our North American premium installed base growing for the 25th consecutive quarter. Further, Grover continues to scale across both existing and new markets. We’re encouraged by the momentum heading into the fourth quarter, with positive feedback from customers post trade shows, customer purchase intentions and recent industry data. To maximize both customer and shareholder value, we expect to leverage this momentum while staying focused on disciplined execution that drives strong cash flows and allows us to both reinvest in product innovation and talent and further reduce leverage to deliver on our commitment.”
Oliver Chow, Chief Financial Officer of Light & Wonder, said, “Our recurring revenue(3) base and margin discipline delivered strong cash generation, with year-to-date Adjusted free cash flow(2) of $545 million, up 49% year-over-year and in line with our full-year 2025 level. As signaled last quarter, we prioritized deleveraging over share repurchases, reducing our net debt leverage ratio(2) by approximately 0.1x sequentially, keeping us on track to fall below 3.0x during the first half of 2027(4). Importantly, we’re also investing deliberately across R&D and Gaming operations capital expenditures, as well as in AI and infrastructure to support both growth and efficiency across the business over time. We remain committed to prioritizing this deleveraging path in the fourth quarter and beyond, as we continue progressing toward our long-term financial targets(4).”

LEVERAGE, CAPITAL ALLOCATION AND BUSINESS UPDATE
•Principal face value of debt outstanding(1) was $5.0 billion, translating to a net debt leverage ratio(2) of 3.3x as of September 30, 2026, representing a 0.1x reduction from June 30, 2026 (3.4x), as we remain committed and on track to reduce our net debt leverage ratio(2) to below 3.0x during the first half of 2027(3).
•Returned $156 million of capital to shareholders year to date through the repurchase of approximately 1.8 million CHESS Depositary Interests (“CDIs”), unchanged from the previous quarter as no shares or CDIs were repurchased through our share repurchase program in the third quarter. Since initiation of the prior share repurchase program in March of 2022, the Company has returned $2.1 billion to shareholders through the repurchase of 26.2 million shares or CDIs. This represents 27% of total outstanding shares prior to the commencement of the programs. With approximately 88% of the current authorized share repurchase program utilized, we have remaining capacity of approximately $180 million(4).
Independent of our share repurchase program, during the third quarter we purchased 0.4 million CDIs on-market for approximately $30 million that are being held in trust by the Company’s employee equity plan administrator for future settlement of equity-based awards. This initiative is designed to offset dilution from vesting of existing and future equity awards.
•FY 2026 financial outlook: Full-year Consolidated AEBITDA(2) growth is expected to be toward the lower end of our previously announced range of mid-to-high single digits(3), with Adjusted NPATA(2) growing faster than Consolidated AEBITDA(3), and EPSa(2) growing faster than Adjusted NPATA(3) as we benefit from previous share buy-back activities. The outlook reflects weakness in the social casino market and some delayed timing of investments and capital expenditures of our customer base, partially offset by our growing recurring revenue(5) base and an expected seasonally strong fourth quarter.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
•Third quarter consolidated revenue decreased to $824 million as compared to $841 million, a 2% decrease versus the prior year period.
◦Gaming revenue performance is outlined below:
▪Gaming operations revenue, inclusive of Grover, increased 3% to $248 million, reflective of underlying growth in North American installed base and average daily revenue per unit;
▪Gaming machine sales increased 5% to $199 million as unit shipment timing normalized, with acceleration expected in the fourth quarter;
▪Gaming systems revenue declined 24% to $55 million, primarily due to lower hardware sales; and
▪Table products revenue fell 7% to $52 million, driven by timing of utility sales.
◦iGaming once again delivered double-digit quarterly revenue growth, increasing 13% compared to the prior year period, driven by 1PP content proliferation and partner network growth in North America.
◦SciPlay revenues decreased 12%, continuing to reflect ongoing softness of the broader social casino free-to-play market, partially offset by continued DTC revenue growth compared to the prior year period, expanding to 31% of total SciPlay revenue, as well as year-over-year improvement in average monthly revenue per paying user, up 5%.
•Net income was $133 million as compared to $114 million, a 17% increase from the prior year period, primarily driven by margin strength on our favorable revenue mix and ongoing operational efficiencies. Net income margin was up 2 percentage points to 16%.
Net income per share(1) was $1.72, compared to $1.34 in the prior year period, a 28% increase year-over-year.
•Consolidated AEBITDA(2) was $383 million, compared to $375 million in the prior year period. The 2% increase was driven by favorable revenue mix shifts and ongoing operational efficiencies that led to continued margin strength, with Consolidated AEBITDA margin(2) up 1 percentage point to 46%. Consolidated AEBITDA(2) includes Corporate and other costs, which are not allocated to the reportable business segments, of $33 million and $35 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, respectively.
•Adjusted NPATA(2) increased 7% to $163 million, versus $153 million in the prior year period, primarily benefiting from Consolidated AEBITDA(2) growth, strong margins and lower interest and stock-based compensation expense.
Adjusted NPATA per share (EPSa)(1)(2) increased 17% to $2.12, compared to $1.81 in the prior year period.
•Net cash provided by operating activities increased 28% to $236 million, compared to $184 million in the prior year period, reflecting strong underlying earnings generation, favorable timing of receivable collections and expenditures, and lower interest payments. The prior year period also had $13 million in costs related to the Grover acquisition and transition to an Australian Securities Exchange (“ASX”) sole primary listing.
•Adjusted free cash flow(2) was $181 million, compared to $149 million in the prior year period, a 21% increase. This reflects strong underlying earnings generation, favorable timing of receivable collections and expenditures, and lower interest payments. Capital expenditures were $90 million, compared to $79 million in the prior year period, driven predominantly by investments supporting Gaming operations growth, including Grover.

Third Quarter 2026 Business Segments Key Highlights
•    Gaming revenue was $554 million, down 1% compared to the prior year period.
◦Gaming operations grew $7 million, or 3%, benefiting from an increase in our North American installed base of 989 units(1), up 3% year-over-year to 36,974 units(1), including a more favorable mix of premium units.
▪Our North American premium installed base grew for the 25th consecutive quarter, with a unit increase of over 2,650 units(1) on a year-over-year basis or 571 units(1) on a sequential quarter-over-quarter basis, and now represents 58% of our total North American installed base mix(1).
▪Our diversified portfolio of successful game franchises and the continued proliferation of our COSMIC® lineup, LIGHTWAVE® and LIGHTWAVE SOLARTM cabinets continued to drive growth and strong performance.
▪Grover benefited from a sequential increase of 205 installed base units, and at period end it had over 12,750 units installed.
◦Gaming machine sales revenue increased 5% as unit shipment timing normalized, with further acceleration expected in the fourth quarter.
◦Gaming systems revenue decreased by 24%, primarily due to lower hardware sales.
◦Table products revenue decreased 7% due to timing of global utility sales.
◦AEBITDA was $312 million, up 2% compared to the prior year period due to favorable revenue mix shifts, a growing recurring revenue(2) base and ongoing operational efficiencies. Gaming margin expanded 1 percentage point to 56%.
•    SciPlay revenue was $173 million, down 12% compared to the prior year period. This was driven by continued softness in the social casino free-to-play market.
◦Daily Active Users, or DAU, remained relatively flat sequentially, while monetization remains a key focus, as AMRPPU(3) grew 5% year-over-year to $132.80.
◦DTC contributed $53 million in revenues, or 31% of SciPlay revenue for the quarter, compared to 20% and 29% for Q3 2025 and Q2 2026, respectively.
◦AEBITDA decreased 6% to $67 million, while margin increased 3 percentage points, primarily driven by margin enhancement initiatives and ongoing growth of the DTC platform.
•    iGaming revenue increased 13% to $97 million, representing another consecutive quarter of double-digit year-over-year revenue growth. The strong performance was driven by continued momentum in North America, underpinned by 1PP content proliferation and the expansion of our partner network.
◦AEBITDA increased 9% to $37 million, while margin decreased 2 percentage points to 38%, reflecting revenue mix changes and growth investment costs.
◦Wagers processed through our iGaming platform reached a quarterly record of $31.7 billion.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Management uses the following non-GAAP financial measures in conjunction with GAAP financial measures: Adjusted NPAT, Adjusted NPATA, Adjusted NPATA per share (on a diluted basis) (also referred to as EPSa), Normalized EBITA, Normalized EBITDA, Consolidated AEBITDA, Grover Adjusted EBITDA, Combined AEBITDA, Consolidated AEBITDA margin, Free cash flow, Adjusted free cash flow, Net debt, Net debt leverage ratio and Combined net debt leverage ratio (each, as described more fully below). These non-GAAP financial measures are presented as supplemental disclosures. They should not be considered in isolation of, as a substitute for, or superior to, the financial information prepared in accordance with GAAP and should be read in conjunction with the Company’s financial statements filed with the SEC and lodged with the ASX. The non-GAAP financial measures used by the Company may differ from similarly titled measures presented by other companies.
Following our transition to a sole primary listing on the ASX, Management introduced usage of Adjusted NPAT, Adjusted NPATA, Adjusted NPATA per share (EPSa), Normalized EBITA and Normalized EBITDA, all of which are non-GAAP financial measures and are widely used to measure the performance as well as a principal basis for valuation of gaming and other companies listed on the ASX.
Specifically, Management uses Consolidated AEBITDA to, among other things: (i) monitor and evaluate the performance of the Company’s operations; (ii) facilitate Management’s internal and external comparisons of the Company’s consolidated historical operating performance; and (iii) analyze and evaluate financial and strategic planning decisions regarding future operating investments and operating budgets.
In addition, Management uses Consolidated AEBITDA and Consolidated AEBITDA margin to facilitate its external comparisons of the Company’s consolidated results to the historical operating performance of other companies that may have different capital structures and debt levels.
Following the closing of the Grover acquisition, Management introduced usage of certain of these non-GAAP financial measures on a “Combined” basis. Combined non-GAAP financial measures include results for both the Company and Grover on a combined basis, inclusive of periods prior to the closing of the acquisition. The Combined measures do not reflect any pro forma adjustments or other adjustments for costs related to integration activities, cost savings or other synergies that have been or may have been achieved if the business combination occurred as of the beginning of the applicable twelve-month period. We cannot assure you that such measures would not be materially different if such information were audited or that our actual results would not differ materially from the Combined measures if the acquisition had been completed as of the beginning of the applicable twelve-month period.
Management uses Net debt, Net debt leverage ratio and Combined net debt leverage ratio in monitoring and evaluating the Company’s overall liquidity, financial flexibility and leverage.
Management believes that these non-GAAP financial measures are useful as they provide Management and investors with information regarding the Company’s financial condition and operating performance that is an integral part of Management’s reporting and planning processes. In particular, Management believes Adjusted NPAT, Adjusted NPATA, Adjusted NPATA per share, Normalized EBITA and Normalized EBITDA are useful for investors because they provide investors with additional perspective on performance, as the measures eliminate the effects of, as applicable, amortization of acquired intangible assets, restructuring, transaction, integration, certain other items, and the income tax impact on such adjustments, which Management believes are less indicative of the ongoing underlying performance of operations and are better evaluated separately. These measures are widely used to measure performance of gaming and other companies listed on the ASX.
Management believes that Consolidated AEBITDA is helpful because this non-GAAP financial measure eliminates the effects of restructuring, transaction, integration or other items that Management believes are less indicative of the ongoing underlying performance of the Company’s operations (as more fully described below) and are better evaluated separately. Management believes that Free cash flow and Adjusted free cash flow provide useful information regarding the Company’s liquidity and its ability to service debt and fund investments.
Management believes that the Combined measures are useful to investors because they provide additional information regarding the combined business of the Company and Grover across the periods being presented, allowing for more meaningful comparisons of overall liquidity, financial flexibility and leverage.
Management also believes that Free cash flow and Adjusted free cash flow are useful for investors because they provide investors with important perspectives on the cash available for debt repayment and other strategic measures, after making necessary capital investments in property and equipment, necessary license payments to support the ongoing business operations, adjustments for changes in restricted cash impacting working capital, and, in the case of Adjusted free cash flow, further adjustments for legal settlements and strategic initiatives cash payments.
