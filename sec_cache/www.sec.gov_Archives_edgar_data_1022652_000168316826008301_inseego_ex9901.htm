Inseego Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Q3 2026 revenue of $33.3 million

 

Q3 2026 Adjusted EBITDA* of Negative $1.0 million and GAAP Net Loss of $8.1 million

 

Announced completion of the acquisition of Nokia’s Fixed Wireless Access Business (“FastMile”) on October 1st

 

SAN DIEGO, November 4, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Inseego Corp. (Nasdaq: INSG) (the “Company”), today reported its results for the third quarter of 2026, ended September 30, 2026.

 

“We were very pleased to complete the acquisition of Nokia’s Fixed Wireless Access Business on October 1st, which will allow us to expand our carrier partnerships globally,” said Juho Sarvikas, CEO of Inseego. “Inseego North America, representing our legacy business, delivered Q3 revenue and adjusted EBITDA within our guided range, but we need to continue to improve our performance. I have taken steps to address the engineering and product delivery issues I highlighted last quarter, including bringing in a new CTO, and we are underway with our efforts to restructure the engineering function. Our focus now is on ensuring execution in the short-term while we integrate the FastMile business with Inseego North America to operate as one global company.”

 

Steven Gatoff, CFO of Inseego, added: “We are pleased to welcome Nokia as an investor in Inseego as a result of the closing of the transaction, and with the support they are providing the business while we integrate our global engineering operations. We have reduced headcount and spend in Q4, which will help move us toward a return to profitability as we look to drive revenue growth throughout the portfolio.”

 

Q3 2026 Financial Highlights

 

·Total revenue for Q3 2026 was $33.3 million.
   
·Adjusted EBITDA* for Q3 2026 was negative $1.0 million. GAAP Net Loss was $8.1 million.
   
·GAAP gross margin for Q3 2026 was 33.3%.

 

Note: Q3 2026 Financial results are for our legacy business only, which we refer to as Inseego North America.

 

Business Highlights

 

·Completed the acquisition of Nokia’s Fixed Wireless Access business (“FastMile”), see separate section below.
   
·Selected by a Tier-1 Carrier to provide a ruggedized, high-powered premium-tier MiFi, with shipments expected to begin in mid-2027.
   
·Introduced intelligent multi-WAN control software for its portfolio of SD-WAN appliances, making resilient connectivity across multiple broadband sources simpler and more accessible for operators and their customers. The capability provides intelligent traffic handling across outdoor 5G receivers, low Earth orbit satellite terminals, fiber, other wired broadband connections, and integrated cellular WAN links.

Nokia FWA Acquisition

 

On October 1st, the Company announced the completion of its acquisition of Nokia’s Fixed Wireless Access (FWA) business, first announced on April 30, 2026. The acquisition is expected to approximately double Inseego’s revenue and position the company as a global wireless broadband leader. By adding indoor, outdoor and millimeter-wave FWA solutions to its wireless broadband portfolio, Inseego expands its reach across consumer and business connectivity. The transaction also meaningfully extends Inseego’s footprint globally to now include leading carriers across Europe, the Middle East, Asia, and Oceania. The addition of this product and technology portfolio also significantly expands Inseego’s engineering capabilities.

 

Following the transaction, approximately 250 people associated with the acquired business are now supporting Inseego’s expanded global operations across engineering, product management, supply chain and sourcing, and customer support. This group includes employees joining Inseego and Nokia personnel who will continue to support the business under a transition services agreement.

 

To support its expanded global operations, Inseego has established an international headquarters in Amsterdam and a development center in Athens, expanded its presence in Bangalore, and added customer-facing sales and technical resources across its new markets.

 

The results of the acquired business will be included in Inseego’s consolidated results beginning in the 4th quarter of 2026. Pro forma information about the revenue of the acquired business is included in a table to this Press Release.

 

Q4 2026 Guidance

 

The Company provided the following guidance for the fourth quarter of 2026:

 

–Q4 2026 total revenue in the range of $56 million to $65 million, comprised of:

 

–Inseego North America revenue in the range of $21 million - $25 million.
   
–Acquired FastMile revenue in the range of $35 million - $40 million.

 

–Q4 2026 Adjusted EBITDA* in the range of $0 million to $2 million. Anticipated Q4 2026 Adjusted EBITDA includes the impact of support payments from Nokia under the acquisition agreement in the range of $12 million to $14 million.

 

The above guidance updates and supersedes any and all prior issued guidance.

About Inseego Corp.

 

Inseego is a global leader in wireless broadband, delivering fast, reliable connectivity to homes, businesses, and people on the move. With the acquisition of Nokia’s FastMile fixed wireless access business, Inseego offers one of the industry’s broadest cellular broadband portfolios, spanning fixed wireless gateways, mobile hotspots and routers, enterprise and industrial gateways, and cloud-based device, network, and subscriber management software. Backed by decades of wireless engineering expertise across 5G, Wi-Fi, antenna design, and cloud software, Inseego partners with mobile network operators worldwide to deliver connectivity at scale. Headquartered in San Diego, California, Inseego has offices in Amsterdam, Bangalore, and Athens. Learn more at www.inseego.com.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

Inseego Corp. has provided financial information in this press release that has not been prepared in accordance with GAAP. Non-GAAP net income (loss) and non-GAAP net income (loss) per share, for example, exclude the impact of share-based compensation expense, impairment of capitalized software, amortization of intangible assets purchased through acquisitions, non-recurring transaction related costs, and other non-recurring gains and losses. Adjusted EBITDA, in addition to those items excluded from non-GAAP net income (loss), excludes all interest expense, taxes, depreciation, amortization, and other non-operating income/expense.

 

Non-GAAP net income (loss), non-GAAP net income (loss) per share, and Adjusted EBITDA are supplemental measures of our performance that are not required by, or presented in accordance with, GAAP. These non-GAAP financial measures have limitations as an analytical tool. They are not intended to be used in isolation or as a substitute for cost of revenues, operating expenses, net income (loss), net income (loss) per share or any other performance measure determined in accordance with GAAP. We present these non-GAAP financial measures because we consider them to be important supplemental performance measures.

We use these non-GAAP financial measures to make operational decisions, evaluate our performance, prepare forecasts and determine compensation. Further, management and investors benefit from referring to these non-GAAP financial measures in assessing our performance when planning, forecasting and analyzing future periods. Share-based compensation expenses are expected to vary depending on the number of new incentive award grants issued to both current and new employees, the number of such grants forfeited by former employees, and changes in our stock price, stock market volatility, expected option term and risk-free interest rates, all of which are difficult to estimate. We believe that these non-GAAP financial measures are helpful to investors in providing greater transparency to the information used by management in its operational decision-making. The Company believes that using these non-GAAP financial measures also facilitates comparing our underlying operating performance with other companies in our industry, which use similar non-GAAP financial measures to supplement their GAAP results.

Investors and potential investors are cautioned that material limitations are associated with using non-GAAP financial measures as an analytical tool. The limitations of relying on non-GAAP financial measures include, but are not limited to, the fact that other companies, including other companies in our industry, may calculate non-GAAP financial measures differently than we do, limiting their usefulness as a comparative tool.
