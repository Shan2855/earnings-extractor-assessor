Tigo Energy Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

LOS GATOS, Calif. – November 4, 2026 – Tigo Energy, Inc. (“Tigo” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: TYGO), a leading provider of intelligent solar and energy solutions, today reported unaudited financial results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026, financial guidance for the fourth quarter ending December 31, 2026, and an updated full-year 2026 outlook.

 

Recent Financial and Operational Highlights

 

●Revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $24.0 million, down 21.5% compared with the third quarter of 2025.

 

●GAAP net income was $0.4 million, compared with a GAAP net loss of $2.2 million in the third quarter of 2025.

 

●Adjusted EBITDA was $1.5 million, compared with adjusted EBITDA of $2.9 million in the third quarter of 2025.

 

●Gross margin was 46.2%, compared with 42.7% in the third quarter of 2025.

 

●Cash and cash equivalents were $16.8 million as of September 30, 2026, compared with $7.7 million as of December 31, 2025.

 

●Long-term debt was $2.5 million as of September 30, 2026, compared with no debt as of December 31, 2025.

 

●During the third quarter of 2026, we shipped 698 thousand units, or 517 MW, of Module Level Power Electronics (“MLPE”).

 

Management Commentary

 

“Third-quarter revenue of $24.0 million was within our guidance, and we delivered GAAP net income of $0.4 million. Adjusted EBITDA of $1.5 million exceeded the high end of our guidance range,” said Zvi Alon, Chairman and CEO of Tigo. “Third-quarter results reflected the three dynamics we outlined last quarter: a more gradual market recovery in Europe, a slower-than-anticipated ramp of GO ESS and the shift of our U.S. optimized inverter partner’s launch to the fourth quarter. In EMEA, third-quarter revenue declined 14.8% year over year as the market continues to adjust to a reduced subsidy environment. GO ESS third-quarter revenue declined 58.6% year over year to $1.3 million and 40.6% compared with the second quarter, due to lower sales volumes in the United States and Italy. In the U.S., we recognized approximately $0.8 million of revenue from shipments to our optimized inverter partner during the quarter and expect revenues to exceed that amount in the fourth quarter. However, the partner’s delayed go-to-market launch may affect the timing of future orders.”

 

“EMEA represented 76.5% of third-quarter revenue, with the Czech Republic and Germany, our two largest markets, representing 19.6% and 19.3% of revenue, respectively. Poland and Spain grew 41.2% and 103.2% year over year, respectively, while revenue declined most significantly in Italy and the United Kingdom. The Americas and LATAM accounted for a combined 16.5% of revenue, with U.S. revenue down 51.4% year over year as the U.S. market adjusts to the expiration of the IRS Section 25D tax credit at the end of 2025. APAC contributed 7.0% of revenue, and APAC revenue grew 56.3% year over year, led by 121.1% growth in Australia. MLPE represented $22.1 million, or 92.0% of quarterly revenue, and GO ESS represented 5.4%. In 2027, we plan to invest in growing markets where we are already succeeding, such as Eastern Europe and Australia, to expand our market share.”

 

 

 

 

 

 

“Gross margin was 46.2%, compared with 42.7% in the third quarter of 2025, and operating expenses declined 10.0% year over year to $11.2 million,” stated Bill Roeschlein, CFO of Tigo. “We reduced inventory by $13.2 million to $18.1 million since year-end 2025. Our 2027 growth plan includes investments in key growth markets while maintaining a disciplined cost structure that can scale with revenue.”

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Results compare the 2026 fiscal third quarter ended September 30, 2026 with the 2025 fiscal third quarter ended September 30, 2025, unless otherwise indicated.

 

●Revenue totaled $24.0 million, compared with $30.6 million.

 

●Gross profit totaled $11.1 million, or 46.2% of net revenue, compared with gross profit of $13.1 million, or 42.7% of net revenue. Third quarter 2026 includes a $0.8 million tariff refund related to prior periods.

 

●Operating expenses totaled $11.2 million, compared with $12.4 million.

 

●Loss from operations totaled $0.1 million, compared with income from operations of $0.6 million.

 

●GAAP net income totaled $0.4 million, compared with a GAAP net loss of $2.2 million.

 

●Non-GAAP net income totaled $1.6 million, compared with a non-GAAP net loss of $0.3 million.

 

●Adjusted EBITDA totaled $1.5 million, compared with adjusted EBITDA of $2.9 million. Third quarter 2026 includes a $0.8 million tariff refund related to prior periods and $0.4 million of bad debt expense, including a charge related to a single European customer.

 

Fourth Quarter 2026 Financial Guidance and Full Year 2026 Outlook

 

The Company provides guidance for the fourth quarter ending December 31, 2026 as follows:

 

●Revenue is expected to be within the range of $20 million to $22 million.

 

●Adjusted EBITDA (loss) is expected to be within the range of $(3.0) million to $(1.5) million.

 

For the full year 2026, the Company is updating its revenue outlook to a range of $95 million to $97 million.

 

“Our fourth-quarter outlook reflects a continuation of the dynamics that affected the third quarter,” added Bill Roeschlein. “With concentrated investments in growing regions where Tigo is seeing success, coupled with a scalable cost structure, we believe we are well positioned to deliver profitable growth as volumes recover.”

 

Actual results may differ materially from the Company’s guidance as a result of, among other things, the factors described below under “Forward-Looking Statements.”

About Tigo Energy, Inc.

 

Founded in 2007, Tigo is a worldwide leader in the development and manufacture of smart hardware and software solutions that enhance safety, increase energy yield, and lower operating costs of residential, commercial, and utility-scale solar systems. Tigo combines its Flex MLPE (Module Level Power Electronics) and solar optimizer technology with intelligent, cloud-based software capabilities for advanced energy monitoring and control. Tigo MLPE products maximize performance, enable real-time energy monitoring, and provide code-required rapid shutdown at the module level. The Company also develops and manufactures products such as inverters and battery storage systems for the residential solar-plus-storage market. For more information, please visit www.tigoenergy.com.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

To supplement our condensed consolidated financial statements, which are prepared and presented in accordance with GAAP, we use the following non-GAAP financial measures: non-GAAP net income (loss) and adjusted EBITDA. The presentation of these financial measures is not intended to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for, or superior to, the financial information prepared and presented in accordance with GAAP.

 

We use adjusted EBITDA and non-GAAP net income (loss) for financial and operational decision-making and as a means to evaluate period-to-period comparisons. We define adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP financial measure, as earnings (loss) before interest and other expenses, net, income tax expense (benefit), depreciation and amortization, as adjusted to exclude stock-based compensation and merger transaction-related expenses. We define non-GAAP net income (loss) as GAAP net income (loss) excluding stock-based compensation. We believe that adjusted EBITDA and non-GAAP net income (loss) provide helpful supplemental information regarding our performance by excluding certain items that may not be indicative of our core business operating results. We believe that both management and investors benefit from referring to adjusted EBITDA and non-GAAP net income (loss) in assessing our performance and when planning, forecasting and analyzing future periods. Adjusted EBITDA and non-GAAP net income (loss) also facilitate management’s internal comparisons to our historical performance and comparisons to our competitors’ operating results. We believe adjusted EBITDA and non-GAAP net income (loss) are useful to investors because they (i) allow for greater transparency with respect to key metrics used by management in its financial and operational decision-making and (ii) are used by our institutional investors and the analyst community to help them analyze the health of our business.

 

The items excluded from adjusted EBITDA and non-GAAP net income (loss) may have a material impact on our financial results. Certain of those items are non-recurring, while others are non-cash in nature. Accordingly, adjusted EBITDA and non-GAAP net income (loss) are presented as supplemental disclosure and should not be considered in isolation from, as a substitute for, or superior to, the financial information prepared in accordance with GAAP.

 

There are a number of limitations related to the use of non-GAAP financial measures. We compensate for these limitations by providing specific information regarding the GAAP amounts excluded from these non-GAAP financial measures and evaluating these non-GAAP financial measures together with their relevant financial measures in accordance with GAAP.

 

We refer investors to the reconciliations of adjusted EBITDA and non-GAAP net income (loss) to net income (loss) included below. A reconciliation for adjusted EBITDA provided as guidance is not provided because, as a forward-looking statement, such reconciliation is not available without unreasonable effort due to the high variability, complexity and difficulty of estimating certain items, such as stock-based compensation expense and currency fluctuations, which could have an impact on our consolidated results.
