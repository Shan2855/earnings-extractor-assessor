RYAM Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Delivers 30% sequential growth in Adjusted EBITDA
Temiscaming production economically idled following U.S. tariffs
•Loss from Continuing Operations of $229 million adjusted for non-cash impairments of $221 million was $8 million, a 60% sequential improvement
•HPC income from continuing operations increased sequentially from $29 million to $44 million, with Adjusted EBITDA of $73 million; total cellulose shipments increased 4%
•Temiscaming paperboard and high yield pulp production economically idled October 3; all site options under evaluation
•Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA of $52 million, inclusive of $2 million in tariff-related costs; year-to-date Cash Flow from Operating Activities of $66 million and Adjusted Free Cash Flow of $2 million
•Strategic review continues with interested parties; tariff-related uncertainty at Temiscaming has slowed the process and we are now targeting a conclusion in Q1 2027
JACKSONVILLE, Fla., November 3, 2026 — Rayonier Advanced Materials Inc. (NYSE:RYAM) (the “Company”) today reported results for its third quarter ended September 26, 2026.
“RYAM delivered another strong quarter in HPC, with higher earnings, increased cellulose shipments and positive cash generation building on the progress we have made since the first quarter,” said Dan Krawczyk, President and Chief Executive Officer. “My time with customers and our teams has reinforced my confidence in the strength of our Cellulose Specialties franchise. Our commodity production mix currently masks the full value of that franchise and dilutes our average realized cellulose price. Our CS products and technical expertise support customers’ manufacturing efficiency and differentiate their finished products. Commodity losses continue to weigh on HPC’s overall results, making a production mix more heavily weighted toward CS a clear and urgent priority. We remain focused on growing CS volumes, loading plant production capacity more heavily toward CS and capturing product value, alongside continued operating improvements. We also see opportunities to create long-term value by leveraging our existing infrastructure, including through the Altamaha Green Energy project at Jesup, where construction is underway.
“Since joining RYAM, I have made advancing the strategic review a priority, and we continue to work actively with interested parties. U.S. tariffs on Canadian paperboard and the resulting October 3 economic idling at Temiscaming have slowed the review as parties seek greater clarity on the site’s path forward and its implications for the broader business. We are managing site costs and evaluating all options for Temiscaming. We are actively providing additional information to help interested parties assess the full value of RYAM, while discussing strategic alternatives to strengthen our CS franchise more quickly. Our mandate is to maximize shareholder value, and we are continuing to strengthen the business throughout the review. Given the complexities of these discussions, we are targeting a conclusion of the strategic review in Q1 2027.”

High Purity Cellulose
Net Sales
Net sales for the third quarter increased $17 million, or 6%, compared to the same prior year quarter, driven by:
•Total Cellulose sales volume increase of 10%, including a 42% increase in CC sales volume that was offset by an 18% decrease in CS sales volume.
–CC sales volume increased due to lower orders for CS products and resulting production shifts to CC, as well as higher uses of inventory in the current quarter.
–CS sales volume declined as the Company executed its CS leadership initiatives and also experienced weaker demand for ethers and other CS end uses.
•Total Cellulose average sales price decrease of 3%, including an 8% decrease in CC average sales price that was partially offset by a 19% increase in CS average sales price.
–CS average sales price increase was driven by higher pricing through CS leadership initiatives in 2026.
–CC average sales price decline was due to softer global commodity pricing and product mix within the commodity portfolio.
•Decrease of $5 million in Biomaterials & Other driven by a prospective reclass, beginning in 2026, of electricity sales from “net sales” to “cost of sales.”

Operating Income
Operating income for the third quarter increased $5 million, or 13%, compared to the same prior year quarter, driven by:
•Increase in CS average sales price.
•Lower wood costs.
•Lower logistics costs.
•Lower maintenance and other service costs due to reduced discretionary spending.
•Insurance recovery of $3 million in the current quarter related to the 2024 Jesup plant fire.
Partially offsetting these income drivers were:
•Lower CS sales volumes and mix resulting from higher CC sales.
•Lower CC pricing and mix.
•Higher chemicals costs.
•Higher energy costs.
Paperboard & High Yield Pulp
Net Sales
Net sales for the third quarter increased $13 million, or 23%, compared to the same prior year quarter, driven by:
•Total sales volume increase of 26%, including 29% and 23% increases for PBD and HYP, respectively, due to:
–Higher sales of folding packaging PBD grades due to increased focus on this market segment.
–Higher sales of lottery PBD grades.
–Increased demand for HYP maple grade in Asia.
–Challenges with HYP supply from Indonesia due to concerns about forestry practices.
Partially offsetting these sales drivers were:
•Total average sales price decrease of 2%, including a 4% decrease for PBD and a flat average sales price for HYP, driven by product mix in PBD.
Operating Loss
The 50% U.S. tariff on Canadian paperboard, effective August 22, 2026, resulted in $2 million of tariff-related costs during the third quarter. Despite mitigation efforts, the tariff rendered continued paperboard and high yield pulp production at Temiscaming uneconomic and operations were economically idled on October 3, 2026. Production remains idled, with estimated site carrying costs of $2 million to $3 million per month. The Company is managing these costs and continues to evaluate all options for the site as part of its strategic review.
Operating loss for the third quarter increased $210 million compared to the same prior year quarter, driven by:
•PBD & HYP non-cash asset impairments of $210 million.
•Decrease in PBD average sales price discussed above.
•Higher wood costs.
•Higher chemicals costs.
•Higher logistics costs.
Partially offsetting these negative impacts were:
•Increases in PBD and HYP sales volumes discussed above.
•Lower costs due to prior year market-driven downtime with no similar downtime in the current quarter.

Corporate & Other
Operating loss for the third quarter was flat compared to the same prior year quarter as a $2 million impairment of the Company’s PBD & HYP assets, higher legal fees and unfavorable foreign exchange rates in the current quarter compared to favorable rates in the prior year quarter were offset by lower variable compensation costs and professional fees.
Liquidity
The Company ended its third quarter with $143 million of global liquidity, including $61 million of cash, $66 million of borrowing capacity under the ABL Credit Facility and $16 million of availability under the France factoring facility.
As of September 26, 2026, the Company’s consolidated net secured leverage ratio was 4.0 times covenant EBITDA.
Year-to-date Adjusted Free Cash Flow was $2 million, an improvement of $10 million from the first-half result. The Company paid $2 million in tariff-related costs on Canadian paperboard during the third quarter.

About RYAM
RYAM is a global leader of high purity cellulose commonly used in the production of filters, food, pharmaceuticals, high performance plastics, propellants and various other industrial applications. RYAM’s specialized assets, capable of creating the world’s leading cellulose specialties products, are also used to produce cellulose viscose pulp, cellulose fluff pulp, paperboard, high yield pulp and various value-added co-products, including biofuels, bioelectricity and lignin. With manufacturing operations in the U.S., Canada and France, RYAM generated $1.5 billion of revenue in 2025. More information is available at www.RYAM.com.
