NI Holdings, Inc. Reports Results for Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2026

 

FARGO, North Dakota, November 6, 2026 – NI Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: NODK) announced today results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Summary of Third Quarter 2026 Results

(All comparisons vs. the third quarter of 2025, unless noted otherwise)

 

·Gross premiums written of $54,346 compared to $58,586 in the prior year quarter. This decrease was driven by Non-Standard Auto (-100.2%) due to the continued impact of the Company’s strategic decision to exit the segment. Also impacting the decrease was a decline in Private Passenger Auto (-8.4%) due to lower new business and renewal premiums in South Dakota and Nebraska and the impact of rate decreases in North Dakota, South Dakota and Nebraska. These declines were partially offset by All Other (+50.6%) due to continued increased assumed premiums from participation on catastrophe reinsurance programs of certain farm bureau insurance companies and Home and Farm (+1.9%) due to strong renewal premiums, rate increases and increased new business in North Dakota.
·Combined ratio of 93.6% versus 109.1% in the prior year quarter. The improvement was primarily driven by favorable prior year development on loss reserves in Non-Standard Auto during the current year quarter compared to unfavorable prior year development in the prior year quarter as well as favorable development on loss reserves for current year storms and lower weather-related losses within the Home and Farm segment. These improvements were slightly offset by higher losses in Crop due to drought conditions in North Dakota as well as increased large loss activity and adverse prior year development on loss reserves in Private Passenger Auto.
·Net investment income of $2.7 million, down 10.7% from the prior year quarter, primarily due to a lower average fixed income portfolio balance as proceeds from maturities and sales were used to fund operating cash needs in the Non-Standard Auto segment.
·Basic earnings per share of $0.27, compared to basic loss per share of $(0.08) in the prior year quarter, reflecting improved profitability for the quarter.

Management Commentary

 

“We delivered significantly improved underwriting results in the third quarter, reflecting continued progress on our strategic priorities,” said Cindy Launer, President and Chief Executive Officer. “We are seeing strong performance in Home and Farm in North Dakota and continued progress in the runoff of Non-Standard Auto. Despite challenges in Crop and Private Passenger Auto, we are encouraged by our momentum and believe we are well positioned to deliver more consistent, profitable performance and long-term shareholder value.”

About the Company

NI Holdings, Inc. is an insurance holding company. The Company is a North Dakota business corporation that is the stock holding company of Nodak Insurance Company and became such in connection with the conversion of Nodak Mutual Insurance Company from a mutual to stock form of organization and the creation of a mutual holding company. The conversion was consummated on March 13, 2017. Immediately following the conversion, all of the outstanding shares of common stock of Nodak Insurance Company were issued to Nodak Mutual Group, Inc., which then contributed the shares to NI Holdings in exchange for 55% of the outstanding shares of common stock of NI Holdings. Nodak Insurance Company then became a wholly-owned stock subsidiary of NI Holdings. NI Holdings’ financial statements are the consolidated financial results of NI Holdings; Nodak Insurance, including Nodak’s wholly-owned subsidiaries American West Insurance Company, Primero Insurance Company, and Battle Creek Insurance Company; and Direct Auto Insurance Company.
