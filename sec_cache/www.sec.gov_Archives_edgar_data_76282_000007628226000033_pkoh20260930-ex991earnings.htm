ParkOhio Announces Record Sales and Strong Third Quarter 2026 Results;
Raises FY 2026 Sales and Adjusted EPS Outlook on Continued Strong Demand Across Its Diversified End Markets

CLEVELAND, OHIO, November 4, 2026 — Park-Ohio Holdings Corp. (NASDAQ: PKOH) today announced its results for the third quarter of 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
•Record revenue of $459 million, up 15% year-over-year.
•Year-over-year sales growth in all three business segments, reflecting continued strong demand across many key end markets, including aerospace and defense, AI data center, electrical steel, and semiconductor.
•Gross margin of 17.9%, up 120 basis points compared to 16.7% in the 2025 third quarter.
•Operating income up 54% year-over-year.
•GAAP EPS of $0.97, up 149% compared to $0.39 in the 2025 third quarter; Adjusted EPS of $1.04, up 60% compared to $0.65 in the prior year quarter and the highest level in two years.
•FY 2026 Sales outlook raised to $1.750 billion to $1.770 billion.
•FY 2026 Adjusted EPS outlook raised to $3.35 to $3.50 per diluted share.
•Operating cash flows of $16 million and free cash flow of $7 million; expect $35 to $45 million of free cash flow in the fourth quarter of 2026.
•Our review of strategic alternatives for our Southwest Steel Processing business is ongoing.

“The global industrial economy, led by North America, continues to underpin accelerating business performance at our Company. Demand has been favorable to expectations throughout the year, and we are pleased to increase both our revenue and earnings outlook again for 2026. While strong demand from semiconductor, electrical infrastructure and aerospace and defense continue to show particular strength, we have seen broad based improvements in the industrial economy and in many of our key markets. As many of these end markets are supply constrained, we expect this strength to continue well into 2027.

“We also recognize the excellent execution by our teammates as we continue to see strengthening margins across the company due not only to additional volume but ongoing productivity improvements,” said Matthew V. Crawford, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer.

Third Quarter 2026 Segment Highlights
•Supply Technologies – Record revenue of $225.2 million, up 21% year-over-year, driven by growth in the semiconductor, AI data center, heavy-duty truck, powersports, and aerospace and defense end markets. The segment continues to expand customized supply-chain management programs, providing global sourcing, specialized production components, inventory management and value-added services to OEM customers. Operating income increased 23%, with operating margins of 9.5%, reflecting higher volumes, operating leverage and ongoing productivity initiatives, including automation. Proprietary fastener sales increased 12%, reflecting demand for engineered fastening technologies.

•Assembly Components – Revenue increased 7% to $103.6 million, driven by new programs and higher demand across automotive platforms. Operating margins improved to 5.4% from 4.8% a year ago. The segment designs and manufactures fluid-routing systems, including engineered hoses, tubing, molded rubber and plastic products and completed assemblies serving traditional, hybrid and electric vehicle applications. Productivity improvements and new product development remain priorities, while the underlying capabilities provide opportunities to serve additional industrial applications over time.

•Engineered Products – Record revenue increased 12% to $129.7 million, with operating income rising to $9.1 million from $3.4 million a year ago. Operating margin improved to 7.0% from 2.9%, reflecting growth in engineered capital equipment, aftermarket parts and services and better operating performance in forged and machined products. New equipment bookings totaled $53 million and equipment backlog reached $242 million at September 30, up 18% from year-end 2025. Global aftermarket capabilities, customized equipment applications used for thermal processing and power management, and productivity initiatives remain important earnings drivers.

Year-to-Date Highlights
•Record revenue of $1.3 billion, up 10% year-over-year.
•Strong year-over-year sales growth in all three business segments.
•Record year-to-date revenues in Supply Technologies and Engineered Products segments.
•Gross margin of 17.7%, an increase of 80 basis points compared to 16.9% in the 2025 period.
•GAAP EPS of $2.41 compared to $1.67 in the 2025 period, up 44%; Adjusted EPS of $2.61 compared to $2.06 in the prior year period, up 27%.

Long-Term Positioning

Our businesses continue to evolve toward a broader mix of end markets and higher-value products and services. We are expanding applications for our engineered technologies, growing aftermarket and service capabilities, and investing in automation, systems and manufacturing productivity. Together, these initiatives are intended to support revenue growth, margin expansion and earnings power over time.
