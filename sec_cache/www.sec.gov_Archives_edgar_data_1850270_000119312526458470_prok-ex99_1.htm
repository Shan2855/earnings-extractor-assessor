ProKidney Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Business Highlights

•
On track to deliver pivotal Phase 3 PROACT 1 surrogate endpoint (eGFR slope) topline results in Q2 2027
•
Anticipate completing full enrollment for the PROACT 1 confirmatory analysis in Q1 2027 with over 500 patients, up from 470, driven by accelerated patient recruitment in mid-2026
•
Presented eight abstracts at ASN Kidney Week 2026, with new data further strengthening the mechanistic and clinical evidence supporting rilparencel; subsequently hosted a KOL event recapping key ASN findings and rilparencel’s potential role in advanced CKD and type 2 diabetes
•
Ended the third quarter with $142 million in cash and cash equivalents and marketable securities, supporting operations into mid-2027

 

WINSTON-SALEM, N.C., November 12, 2026 – ProKidney Corp. (Nasdaq: PROK) (“ProKidney” or the “Company"), a leading late clinical-stage cell therapy company focused on chronic kidney disease (CKD), today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 and provided business highlights and upcoming milestones.

 

“The third quarter marked continued progress for ProKidney, with PROACT 1 advancing toward full enrollment and our pivotal eGFR slope topline readout remaining on track for the second quarter of 2027,” said Bruce Culleton, M.D., CEO of ProKidney. “The data presented at Kidney Week further build the mechanistic and clinical evidence supporting rilparencel and provide a more cohesive picture of how it may stabilize kidney function. Together, these advances reinforce our confidence as we approach this important Phase 3 readout and continue our mission to transform the treatment of advanced CKD.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

 

 

 

Liquidity: Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities as of September 30, 2026, totaled $141.9 million, compared to $181.6 million as of June 30, 2026.

 

R&D Expenses: Research and development expenses were $40.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $26.8 million for the same period in 2025. The increase of $14.0 million was driven by increases in clinical study and related manufacturing costs of $7.4 million, primarily related to the Company’s ongoing PROACT 1 study. Additionally, professional fees and other operational costs increased $1.3 million and $3.5 million, respectively, due to continued enrollment, ongoing activities and preparation for regulatory filings. Finally, compensation costs increased by approximately $1.8 million related to the hiring of additional personnel.

 

G&A Expenses: General and administrative expenses were $13.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $11.9 million for the same period in 2025. The increase of $1.2 million was primarily driven by a $1.7 million increase in compensation costs related to the hiring of additional personnel, partially offset by decreases in professional fees and impairment charges.

 

Net Loss Before Noncontrolling Interest: Net loss before noncontrolling interest was $51.8 million and $35.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, respectively.

 

Shares outstanding: Class A and Class B common stock outstanding at September 30, 2026, totaled 302,371,200.

 

About Chronic Kidney Disease

CKD is a progressive condition characterized by the gradual decline of kidney function, which can ultimately lead to end-stage kidney disease (ESKD), requiring dialysis or transplantation. An estimated 37 million adults in the U.S. have CKD, though many remain undiagnosed in the early stages. Diabetes is the leading cause of CKD, and individuals with both conditions face significantly elevated risks of cardiovascular events, hospitalization, and mortality. ProKidney is developing rilparencel for patients with Stage 3b/4 CKD and diabetes, a population that includes over one million people in the U.S. While current treatment options aim to slow disease progression, there remains a substantial unmet need for therapies that can stabilize kidney function and delay or prevent the need for dialysis in patients with advanced CKD.

 

About the Phase 3 REGEN-006 (PROACT 1) Clinical Trial

REGEN-006 is an ongoing Phase 3, randomized, blinded, sham-controlled safety and efficacy study of rilparencel in subjects with advanced CKD and type 2 diabetes. The study protocol was amended in 1H 2024 to focus on a subset of patients with Stage 4 CKD (eGFR 20-30 mL/min/1.73m2) and late Stage 3b CKD (eGFR 30-35 mL/min/1.73m2) with accompanying albuminuria (UACR less than 5,000 mg/g for patients with eGFR 20-30 mL/min/1.73m2 and 300-5,000 mg/g for patients with eGFR 30-35 mL/min/1.73m2). The Company now expects total enrollment of over 500 subjects. Subjects are randomized (1:1) to the treatment group and the sham control group prior to kidney biopsy or a sham biopsy procedure, respectively. The primary objective is to assess the efficacy of up to two rilparencel injections (one in each kidney) using a minimally invasive percutaneous approach. The surrogate endpoint for accelerated approval is eGFR slope, and the primary composite endpoint is the time from first injection to the earliest of: at least 40% reduction in eGFR; eGFR <15 mL/min/1.73m², and/or chronic dialysis, and/or renal transplant; or renal or cardiovascular death.

 

About ProKidney Corp.

ProKidney, a pioneer in the treatment of CKD through innovations in cell therapy, was founded in 2015 after a decade of research. ProKidney’s lead product candidate, rilparencel (also known as REACT®), is a first-in-class, patented, proprietary autologous cell therapy with regenerative medicine advanced therapy

 

 

designation that is being evaluated in the ongoing Phase 3 REGEN-006 (PROACT 1) study for its potential to preserve kidney function in patients with advanced CKD and type 2 diabetes. For more information, please visit www.prokidney.com.
