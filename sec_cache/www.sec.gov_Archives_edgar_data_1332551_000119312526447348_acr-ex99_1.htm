ACRES COMMERCIAL REALTY CORP.

REPORTS RESULTS FOR

THIRD QUARTER 2026

 

Uniondale, NY, November 4, 2026 – ACRES Commercial Realty Corp. (NYSE: ACR) (“ACR” or the “Company”), a real estate investment trust that is primarily focused on originating, underwriting and managing commercial real estate mortgage loans and that manages third-party capital and provides insurance solutions, today reported results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. ACR’s GAAP net loss allocable to common shares was $56.3 million or $(5.16) per share-diluted, for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

"In the third quarter, ACRES completed the merger transaction with its manager and is now internally managed. Going forward the Company will have three lines of business that management expects to drive results in the future: Principal Investing, Investment Management and Insurance," said ACRES Commercial Realty Corp. Chairman Andrew Fentress. "As an internally managed company, the employees and directors own over 45% of the common shares and are directly aligned with shareholders."

ACR issued a full, detailed presentation of its results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 that can be viewed at www.acresreit.com.

About ACRES Commercial Realty Corp.

ACRES is a public commercial mortgage REIT that is primarily focused on originating, underwriting and managing mortgage loans. ACRES invests nationwide in the middle market with a focus on multifamily, student housing, hospitality, industrial and office properties. In addition, ACRES manages third-party capital and provides insurance solutions through its investment management business. For more information, please visit the Company’s website at www.acresreit.com or contact investor relations at IR@acresreit.com.
