Albemarle Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

CHARLOTTE, N.C. – November 4, 2026 - Albemarle Corporation (NYSE: ALB), a global leader in providing essential elements for mobility, energy, connectivity and health, today announced its results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 and Recent Highlights
(Unless otherwise stated, all percentage changes represent year-over-year comparisons and do not exclude the prior-period results of Ketjen’s refining catalyst solutions business in which the company sold a 51% stake on March 2, 2026)
•Net sales of $1.6 billion, up 19% due to higher pricing in Energy Storage (+68%) and higher pricing and volumes in Specialties (price +10%, volume +3%).
•Net income of $297 million, or $2.15 per diluted share attributable to common shareholders; Adjusted net income(a) of $354 million, or $2.58 per diluted share attributable to common shareholders which primarily excludes restructuring and other related charges.
•Adjusted EBITDA(a) of $646 million; up 186% due primarily to higher pricing in Energy Storage, increased pricing and volumes in Specialties and cost and productivity improvements.
•Cash from operating activities of $406 million and free cash flow of $325 million(a). Operating cash flow conversion of 63%(a), supported by a cash dividend from the Talison joint venture which partly offset timing of working capital changes.
•Delivered more than $150 million in year-to-date run-rate cost and productivity improvements, achieving the high end of our full-year target.
•Talison’s Chemical Grade Plant 3 (CGP3) achieved 30-days of nameplate performance in September.
•Improving full-year 2026 outlook considerations including:
◦Specialties adjusted EBITDA of $350-$375 million (up from $275-$325 million), in part driven by ongoing cost and productivity improvements.
◦Reducing full-year capital expenditure forecast to $300-$350 million (down from approximately $500 million), reflecting continued benefits from our focused capital efficiency improvements, as well as resequencing of some early-stage projects.
(a)    See Non-GAAP Reconciliations for further details.

“Albemarle delivered another solid quarter, reflecting disciplined execution across the enterprise,” said Kent Masters, Chairman and CEO. “In Specialties, improved pricing and sustained progress on cost and productivity initiatives support an additional increased outlook for the year. Global lithium demand is up 48% year-to-date though August, driven by both electric vehicles and energy storage, reinforcing our confidence in the long-term outlook for the industry. We will continue to prioritize high-return organic growth by leveraging our advantaged resource base, capital spending efficiency, and financial flexibility.”

Specialties Outlook Considerations
Specialties adjusted EBITDA outlook is improved as strong year-to-date performance, primarily driven by cost and productivity improvements, more than offset supply chain related cost escalation due to the conflict in the Middle East. Our outlook continues to reflect modest volume growth in key end markets led by semiconductors, oil and gas, flame retardants and pharmaceuticals and steady year-to-date demand in automotive and petrochemical markets. Fourth quarter outlook assumes steady demand in the bromine market and the impact of the ongoing situation in the Middle East.
