Safe Harbor Financial Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Revenue increases 6.5% to $1.95 million, supported by 70.2% growth in loan program income

 

Average client deposits rise to $112 million, with quarter-end trailing 14-day average deposits reaching $119.3 million

 

Operating loss narrows 17.7% year over year

 

GOLDEN, Colo., Nov. 9, 2026 — SHF Holdings, Inc., doing business as Safe Harbor (the “Company” or “Safe Harbor”) (Nasdaq: SHFS), a fintech platform serving the banking, lending and financial services needs of the regulated cannabis and hemp industries, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Safe Harbor reported third-quarter revenue of $1.95 million, an increase of 6.5% compared with $1.83 million in the prior-year period. Revenue growth was primarily driven by a 70.2% increase in loan program income, reflecting improved revenue-sharing economics under the Company’s amended agreement with Partner Colorado Credit Union that more than offset lower average loan portfolio balances.

 

Average client deposit balances increased 3.8% year over year to $112 million, while the trailing 14-day average deposit balance at quarter-end reached $119.3 million, an increase of 7.4% from the prior-year period. The quarter-end trailing 14-day balance was the Company’s highest since April 2024. The Average account balance increased 5.6% from the prior-year period, reflecting higher deposit balances across the Company’s client relationships.

 

Operating loss in the third quarter narrowed 17.7% year over year to approximately $1 million, reflecting higher revenue, lower compensation expenses and a credit benefit associated with changes in estimated credit losses. These benefits were partially offset by increased professional services and other operating costs.

 

“Our third-quarter results demonstrate the continued value of Safe Harbor’s established financial services platform, with higher revenue, significant growth in loan program income and increased client deposit balances,” said Terry Mendez, Chief Executive Officer of Safe Harbor. “Our enhanced lending economics are contributing to revenue growth, while our deposit performance reflects the strength of the client relationships and specialized banking infrastructure we have built over more than a decade.”

 

“At the same time, we recognize the challenges facing our capital structure and public listing and remain focused on evaluating solutions that preserve the value of our operating business,” Mr. Mendez continued. “Our priorities are maintaining service to our clients and financial institution partners, managing liquidity and pursuing strategic opportunities that may support a more sustainable path forward.”

Operating Performance

 

Loan program income increased approximately $359,000, or 70.2%, to approximately $869,000 in the third quarter, compared with approximately $511,000 in the prior-year period.

 

The increase was primarily attributable to Safe Harbor’s second amended commercial alliance agreement with Partner Colorado Credit Union, effective October 1, 2025. The agreement increased Safe Harbor’s share of applicable loan program income from approximately 35% to up to 65%, thereby improving the revenue contribution from its lending services despite lower average outstanding loan balances.

 

For the first nine months of 2026, loan program income increased 58.6% to $2.55 million from $1.61 million in the prior-year period. The increase primarily reflected the revised revenue-sharing arrangement rather than growth in underlying loan volume.

 

Safe Harbor’s deposit base continued to expand during the quarter. Average client deposit balances increased approximately $4.0 million year over year to $112.0 million, compared with approximately $108.0 million in the prior-year period.

 

The trailing 14-day average deposit balance at September 30, 2026, reached $119.3 million, an increase of 7.4% from $111.1 million a year earlier.

 

The Average account balance increased 5.6% to $147,209 from $139,444 in the prior-year period, while average active accounts declined 1.7% to 761 from 774. The increase in average balances reflects a shift toward larger, higher-deposit-balance client relationships.

 

Account fee income decreased 17.1% to approximately $798,000, compared with approximately $963,000 in the prior-year period. The decline primarily reflected lower revenue from a merchant service partner and competitive pressure on account fees.

 

Investment income decreased 16.7% to approximately $284,000, compared with approximately $341,000 a year earlier, as lower prevailing interest rates more than offset growth in average investable deposit balances.

 

Operating Expenses and Profitability

 

Total operating expenses decreased approximately $97,000, or 3.2%, to $2.95 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared with $3.05 million in the prior-year period.

 

The decrease included lower compensation and employee benefits expenses and an approximate $353,000 credit benefit associated with changes in estimated credit losses under the Company’s financial indemnification obligations.

 

These benefits were partially offset by increased professional services expenses and amortization of a contract asset associated with the second amended commercial alliance agreement with Partner Colorado Credit Union.

 

Professional services expenses increased 63.3% to approximately $722,000 from $442,000 a year earlier, reflecting increased legal, accounting and other professional costs.

 

Operating loss narrowed to $1 million from $1.22 million in the third quarter of 2025.

 

Safe Harbor reported a net loss of approximately $979,000, compared with net income of approximately $180,000 in the prior-year quarter. The year-over-year comparison was significantly affected by a $3.34 million gain on debt extinguishment recorded during the third quarter of 2025.

 

For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, Safe Harbor reported total revenue of $5.86 million, an increase of 4.5% from $5.61 million in the comparable 2025 period. Operating loss for the nine-month period narrowed to $3.79 million, compared with $4.18 million a year earlier.

 

 
 

 

Strategic and Business Developments

 

Safe Harbor remains focused on strengthening its core financial services platform, supporting existing client relationships and positioning its institutional banking capabilities to serve a broader range of financial institutions.

 

During the third quarter, the Company continued operating its established banking and lending platform, supported by higher average client deposit balances and increased loan program income. These results reflect the continued contribution of Safe Harbor’s financial institution relationships and specialized experience serving regulated cannabis businesses.

 

The Safe Harbor Institutional Infrastructure-as-a-Service model is an important component of the Company’s longer-term strategy. The model is designed to provide banks and credit unions with operational infrastructure, compliance support and program administration capabilities to help establish and manage cannabis banking programs.

 

During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, Safe Harbor Institutional was recognized as Top Business Solutions Company at the 2026 PBC Cannabis Banking Conference, highlighting its specialized banking infrastructure and compliance capabilities serving the regulated cannabis industry. The recognition further supports the Company’s efforts to raise awareness of its institutional offering among banks and credit unions.

 

Following the end of the quarter, Safe Harbor Institutional joined the American Bankers Association (“ABA”) Partner Network, thereby expanding its opportunities to engage with U.S. banking institutions. Through the network, the Company aims to introduce its specialized cannabis banking infrastructure and compliance capabilities to a broader audience of banking executives and decision-makers.

 

These initiatives support Safe Harbor’s strategy to strengthen its institutional presence, build relationships across the banking industry and identify opportunities to expand its financial services platform. Participation in the ABA Partner Network does not constitute an endorsement of Safe Harbor’s products or services by the ABA.

 

Management remains focused on supporting existing clients and financial institution partners, maintaining operational continuity and preserving the value of its established cannabis financial services business while addressing capital structure and liquidity requirements.

 

Balance Sheet and Liquidity

 

As of September 30, 2026, Safe Harbor reported cash and cash equivalents of $4.40 million, compared with $6.78 million as of December 31, 2025.

 

Total assets were $13.09 million as of September 30, 2026, compared with $17.21 million at year-end 2025.

 

Net cash used in operating activities totaled $4.05 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared with $2.35 million in the prior-year period.

 

The Company had no secured debt as of September 30, 2026.

 

Safe Harbor continues to manage liquidity through cash flow monitoring, expense management and evaluation of available financing and strategic alternatives.

 

The Company does not intend to draw on its existing equity line of credit while the term sheet with Opifex Media Inc. (“Opifex”) remains in effect, which will continue to limit an available source of financing.

 

The Company’s unaudited financial statements include a going concern disclosure resulting from recurring operating losses, negative operating cash flows and uncertainties related to its capital structure, litigation and other obligations. Management’s plans have not alleviated substantial doubt about the Company’s ability to continue as a going concern.

 

 
 

 

Subsequent Developments

 

Proposed Business Combination

 

On October 23, 2026, Safe Harbor entered into a nonbinding term sheet with Opifex, a Beverly Hills, California-based media holding company focused on the creator economy, regarding a proposed business combination.

 

The proposed transaction is intended to create a diversified public holding company with two distinct operating businesses. Opifex would contribute its creator-economy platform, while Safe Harbor would continue operating its established cannabis financial services business and serving its existing clients and financial institution partners.

 

As currently contemplated, Opifex security holders would own approximately 85% of the combined company’s fully diluted shares immediately after closing. Existing Safe Harbor stakeholders, including common stockholders, holders of Series B preferred stock on an as-converted basis, warrant holders and other equity award holders, would collectively own approximately 15% of the combined company. However, the Safe Harbor allocation described above may be adjusted within a range of 12.5% to 17.5% to reflect Safe Harbor’s valuation at closing, as supported by an independent fairness opinion.

 

The proposed transaction is intended to support Safe Harbor’s efforts to maintain its Nasdaq listing and provide an additional business platform for the combined company.

 

The transaction remains subject to, among other things, the negotiation of a definitive agreement, satisfactory due diligence findings by both parties, the required consents and agreements with holders of the Series B securities, the completion of Opifex’s audited financial statements, an independent valuation and fairness opinion, Safe Harbor stockholder approval, Nasdaq approval, completion of any required financing and other customary conditions.

 

Safe Harbor continues to evaluate potential arrangements involving its Series B securities in connection with the proposed transaction.

 

There can be no assurance that definitive agreements will be executed, required approvals will be obtained or the proposed transaction will be completed on the terms described above or at all.

 

Nasdaq Listing Compliance

 

On October 20, 2026, Nasdaq granted Safe Harbor an additional 180-day period, through April 19, 2027, to regain compliance with the $1 minimum bid price requirement.

 

The Company’s continued listing remains subject to applicable Nasdaq requirements, including provisions that may result in an earlier delisting determination if its common stock fails to maintain specified minimum price thresholds. Safe Harbor continues to evaluate available alternatives to address its listing requirements, including measures contemplated in connection with its proposed transaction with Opifex.

 

There can be no assurance that the Company will regain compliance or maintain its Nasdaq listing.

 

Management Priorities

 

Safe Harbor remains focused on maintaining its existing financial services operations and supporting customers and financial institution partners while evaluating alternatives to address its capital structure and public listing challenges.

 

Management’s near-term priorities include supporting client deposits, maintaining banking and lending relationships, evaluating institutional banking opportunities, managing expenses and liquidity, and pursuing strategic alternatives intended to preserve the value of the operating business.

 

“We remain committed to the clients and financial institution partners who rely on our platform,” Mr. Mendez said. “Our focus is on maintaining operational continuity, protecting the capabilities we have built and evaluating opportunities that may provide a more sustainable foundation for the business and its stakeholders.”

About Safe Harbor

 

Safe Harbor is a cannabis-exclusive financial platform delivering smarter banking, lending, payments and business services, and institutional solutions tailored to how the cannabis industry actually operates. As one of the original pioneers of compliant financial operations support and cannabis banking consulting in the U.S., Safe Harbor has assisted in the processing of more than $38.7 billion in cannabis-related depository funds across 41 states and territories since inception. Through its proprietary technology, platform and network of regulated financial institution partners, Safe Harbor empowers cannabis operators to gain clarity, control and confidence in their financial operations. From daily banking and compliance infrastructure to long-term growth, Safe Harbor provides real solutions and personal support, built exclusively for cannabis. Safe Harbor is a financial technology company, not a bank and not a lender. Banking services are provided by our partner financial institutions. Credit decisions are made by third-party financing partners, not Safe Harbor. For more information, visit shfinancial.org.
