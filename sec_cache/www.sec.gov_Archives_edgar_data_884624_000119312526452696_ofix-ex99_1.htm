Orthofix Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results and Revises Full-Year 2026 Guidance

LEWISVILLE, Texas — November 9, 2026 — Orthofix Medical Inc. (NASDAQ:OFIX), a leading global medical technology company, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and revised its full-year 2026 net sales and non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA guidance. Unless otherwise noted, pro forma results exclude the impact of the discontinued M6™ product lines.

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

•
Total reported net sales of $207.9 million, representing an increase of 1% on a reported basis and 2% on a pro forma constant currency basis compared with the prior year period.
•
Reported net loss of $(6.1) million and non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA of $26.0 million.
•
Revised full-year 2026 pro forma net sales guidance to $825 million to $830 million and non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA guidance to $86 million to $88 million, reflecting third-quarter performance and the anticipated fourth quarter impact of actions to improve U.S. Spine execution, instrument set availability and asset productivity.
•
Global Spine Fixation1 net sales increased 1% on a reported and constant currency basis compared with the prior year period.
•
Global Limb Reconstruction net sales increased 8% on a reported basis and 9% on a constant currency basis compared with the prior year period.
•
Therapeutic Solutions net sales increased 4% compared with the prior year period.

“Continued growth in Limb Reconstruction and Therapeutic Solutions helped offset weaker Spine performance,” said Massimo Calafiore, President and Chief Executive Officer. “In U.S. Spine, we are accelerating actions to strengthen commercial execution, improve instrument set supply and allocation, and increase asset productivity by focusing resources on our highest-performing distributor partners, products and market opportunities. Our revised guidance reflects third-quarter performance and the near-term impact of these actions, which are intended to build a stronger foundation for sustainable growth, profitability and cash generation.”

1 Spine Fixation is comprised of the Company’s Spinal Implants product category, excluding motion preservation product offerings.

For the third quarter of 2026, net sales were $207.9 million, representing an increase of 1.1% on a reported basis and 2.3% on a non-GAAP pro forma constant currency basis compared to third quarter 2025.

For the third quarter of 2026, GAAP gross margins were 70.5% and were 70.7% on a non-GAAP adjusted basis.

For the third quarter of 2026, reported net loss was $(6.1) million, or $(0.15) per share compared to reported net loss of $(22.8) million, or $(0.57) per share in the prior year period. Non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA was $26.0 million, or 12.5% of pro forma net sales, in the third quarter of 2026, compared to non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA of $24.6 million, or 12.1% of pro forma net sales, in the third quarter of 2025. The year-over-year increase primarily reflected continued cost discipline and resource alignment initiatives, partially offset by lower Spine revenue, unfavorable geographic mix and the related reduction in operating leverage.

Liquidity

Cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash totaled $86.9 million at September 30, 2026, compared with $104.4 million at June 30, 2026. The sequential change primarily reflected working capital timing.

Business Outlook

Orthofix is revising its full-year 2026 net sales and adjusted EBITDA guidance. Both guidance ranges assume current foreign currency exchange rates.

•
Net sales are now expected to range from $825 million to $830 million, compared with prior guidance of $845 million to $855 million.
•
Non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA guidance is now expected to range from $86 million to $88 million, compared with prior guidance of $95 million to $98 million.

About Orthofix

Orthofix is a global medical technology company dedicated to advancing healing and restoring mobility for patients with complex musculoskeletal conditions. Headquartered in Lewisville, Texas, the Company offers a differentiated portfolio of spinal implants, therapeutic solutions, limb reconstruction systems, biologics and enabling technologies, including the 7D FLASH™ Navigation System. Orthofix’s technology-enabled solutions are designed to support surgeons across the continuum of care and improve outcomes for patients. Learn more at Orthofix.com and follow Orthofix on LinkedIn.
