Alumis Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and
Highlights Recent Business Updates

​

– New Phase 3 data demonstrated rapid and durable improvements in plaque psoriasis burden, adding to envudeucitinib’s differentiated clinical profile, including the highest publicly reported Week 48 PASI 100 response rate among existing and investigational oral psoriasis therapies –

​

– Alumis remains on track to submit an NDA for envudeucitinib in moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis in 4Q 2026 –

​

–Announced topline results from the LUMUS Phase 2b trial for envudeucitinib in systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) –

​

SOUTH SAN FRANCISCO, Calif., November 12, 2026 – Alumis Inc. (Nasdaq: ALMS), a late-stage biopharmaceutical company developing next-generation targeted therapies for patients with immune-mediated diseases, today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, and highlighted recent business updates and upcoming milestones.

​

“Each additional readout from the ONWARD program has strengthened the consistent body of evidence supporting envudeucitinib’s highly competitive profile in moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis,” said Martin Babler, President and Chief Executive Officer of Alumis. “The new data presented in October showed early and durable improvement across scalp disease severity, itch resolution, and quality of life measures, building on a 54% PASI 100 response rate observed at 48 weeks of treatment. Our fourth-quarter 2026 NDA submission remains on track.”

​

Third Quarter 2026 and Recent Highlights

Envudeucitinib: a next-generation, highly selective oral tyrosine kinase 2 (TYK2) inhibitor, in patients with moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis

●New data presented at the 26th Annual Fall Clinical Dermatology Conference demonstrated early and durable improvements across scalp disease severity, itch resolution, and quality of life measures. These data build on envudeucitinib’s high-threshold skin clearance observed across the ONWARD program, including a 54% Psoriasis Area and Severity Index (PASI) 100 response rate at Week 48 (LINK TO PR).
●High rate of scalp clearance: Over 80% of patients in ONWARD3 maintained clear or almost clear scalp skin (scalp-specific Physician Global Assessment [ss-PGA] 0/1) at 48 weeks.
●Rapid, robust and sustained itch relief: In ONWARD1/2, approximately one-third of patients—including those with moderate-to-severe scalp disease (ss-PGA ≥3)—achieved clinically meaningful itch reduction (≥4-point NRS improvement) as early as Week 2. By Week 16, about 40% reached complete itch resolution (NRS 0) and approximately 60% reported minimal or no itch (NRS 0/1). In ONWARD3, nearly 80% of patients sustained clinically meaningful itch reduction through Week 48.

​

●Early, durable quality-of-life improvements: Approximately 50% of patients in ONWARD1/2 achieved a Dermatology Life Quality Index (DLQI) score of 0 or 1 by Week 12, reflecting minimal to no impact of psoriasis on daily activities. In ONWARD3, more than 70% of patients maintained DLQI 0/1 at 48 weeks.
●Rapid crossover convergence: Patients switching from placebo or apremilast achieved rapid improvements across primary and key secondary endpoints, comparable to those continuously treated at Week 48.
●Alumis remains on track to submit its New Drug Application to the FDA for envudeucitinib in moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis in the fourth quarter of 2026.

​

Envudeucitinib: Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE)

●Phase 2b LUMUS trial of envudeucitinib for the treatment of moderate-to-severe systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) did not meet its primary and secondary endpoints in the overall trial population (LINK TO PR).
●Robust clinical responses were observed in a prespecified subgroup analysis of patients with high interferon gene signature (IFNGS-high), including on the primary endpoint of British Isles Lupus Assessment Group-based Composite Lupus Assessment (BICLA) and key secondary endpoints of Cutaneous Lupus Erythematosus Disease Area and Severity Index 50 (CLASI-50), SLE Responder Index 4 (SRI-4), and Lupus Low Disease Activity State (LLDAS).
●IFNGS-high patients represent the majority of moderate-to-severe SLE cases. In LUMUS, they were unexpectedly under-represented, reducing response rates in the overall trial population.
●Pharmacodynamic data from LUMUS confirmed robust dose-dependent interferon-pathway target engagement, with maximal suppression at the highest dose, 40 mg twice daily.
●Envudeucitinib was well tolerated in LUMUS, with no new safety signals.
●Alumis plans to conduct end-of-Phase 2 regulatory interactions and evaluate development options.

​

TYK2 Franchise and Pipeline Update

●Alumis plans to initiate a Phase 2 biomarker trial for A-005, its central nervous system (CNS) penetrant TYK2 inhibitor, in patients with Parkinson’s disease, in 2027. In a Phase 1 healthy volunteer study, A-005 was well-tolerated, achieved maximal target inhibition in the periphery, and demonstrated the ability to penetrate the CNS.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

●As of September 30, 2026, Alumis had cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities of $431.3 million.
●Revenue consisted of collaboration revenue of $1.9 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to collaboration revenue of $2.1 million for the quarter ended

​

September 30, 2025, related to the collaboration and licensing agreement with Kaken Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd.
●Research and development expenses were $80.8 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $97.8 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The decrease was primarily driven by lower clinical trial and contract research costs reflecting the progression of the  envudeucitinib clinical program following completion of the pivotal Phase 3 ONWARD1 and ONWARD2 clinical trials in patients with moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis, partially offset by an increase in clinical trial and contract research costs for the Phase 3 ONWARD3 clinical trial.
●General and administrative expenses were $20.2 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $19.5 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The increase was primarily attributable to costs related to launch preparation efforts and an increase in personnel-related expenses for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, partially offset by severance and transaction costs related to the merger with ACELYRIN, Inc. and legal costs in the quarter ended September 30, 2025.
●Net loss was $94.9 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to net loss of $110.8 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.

​

Financial Guidance

●Based on the Company’s current operating plan, Alumis continues to anticipate that its existing cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities as of September 30, 2026 are sufficient to fund operating expenses and capital expenditure requirements into the fourth quarter of 2027.

About Alumis

Alumis is a late-stage biopharma company developing next-generation targeted therapies with the potential to significantly improve patient health and outcomes across a range of immune-mediated diseases. Leveraging its proprietary data analytics platform and precision approach, Alumis is developing a pipeline of oral tyrosine kinase 2 inhibitors, consisting of envudeucitinib for the treatment of systemic immune-mediated disorders, such as moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis and systemic lupus erythematosus, and A-005 for the treatment of neuroinflammatory and neurodegenerative diseases, such as Parkinson’s disease. In addition, the pipeline includes several preclinical programs identified through this precision approach. For more information, visit www.alumis.com or follow us on LinkedIn or X.
