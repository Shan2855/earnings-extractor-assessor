Peter Sack, Chief Executive Officer of the Company, commented, “The third quarter continued to show that Chicago Atlantic BDC is uniquely positioned to generate attractive risk adjusted returns with a disciplined focus on capital preservation. This has been a record year for gross originations thus far: we have deployed $167.4 million year to date, including, $72.0 million to two new borrowers in the third quarter, growing the portfolio to the largest size in company history. We are one of the only publicly traded BDCs primarily focused on lending to cannabis operators, and to companies in the lower middle market often overlooked by traditional capital providers.”

 

Mr. Sack continued, “Our differentiated investment approach has produced a strong and resilient portfolio characterized by stable risk ratings, no investments on non-accrual, and a 100% senior secured debt portfolio, generating a weighted average yield of 15.5%. Our focus on less competitive, uncorrelated credit opportunities supports attractive risk adjusted returns, and a robust pipeline of differentiated investment opportunities.”

As of September 30, 2026, the Company’s investment portfolio had an aggregate fair value of approximately $399.6 million across 39 portfolio companies. During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, the Company funded two new debt investments to new portfolio companies with an aggregate principal amount of $72.0 million. The Company also received scheduled and unscheduled principal repayments totaling $5.1 million, and sold a loan position for $3.0 million.

 

Subsequent to quarter end, $2.0 million was funded to an existing portfolio company. As of September 30, 2026, there were no loans on non-accrual status.

 

Liquidity and Capital Resources

 

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $10.2 million of liquidity, including $1.2 million of cash and $9.0 million of borrowings available to be drawn on its $100.0 million senior credit facility, which is subject to certain borrowing base requirements and other restrictions. As of November 11, 2026, the Company had $94.5 million outstanding on its senior credit facility and approximately $6.7 million of liquidity.

 

On September 30, 2026, the Company’s shelf registration statement on Form N-2, initially filed on May 11, 2026, was declared effective by the SEC, allowing the Company to offer and sell up to $500 million of securities including debt securities, subject to market conditions and other applicable requirements. The shelf registration statement provides the Company with enhanced financial flexibility to efficiently access the capital markets to grow the Company’s portfolio.

Chicago Atlantic Real Estate Finance Merger Update

 

On June 17, 2026, the Company entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (the “Merger Agreement”) with Chicago Atlantic Real Estate Finance, Inc. (“REFI”) (NASDAQ: REFI), an affiliated company that has elected to be taxed as a real estate investment trust and is externally managed by an affiliate of the Company’s investment adviser, pursuant to which REFI will merge with and into the Company, with the Company continuing as the surviving company (the “Merger”). Prior to the Merger, REFI will elect to be regulated as a business development company under the Investment Company Act of 1940, as amended (the “1940 Act”). At closing, REFI’s stockholders will receive a number of shares of the Company’s common stock determined based on the ratio (the “Exchange Ratio”) of REFI’s net asset value (“NAV”) per share, as adjusted in accordance with the Merger Agreement, to the Company’s NAV per share, similarly adjusted, in each case as determined shortly prior to closing. Based on the respective NAVs of REFI and the Company as of March 31, 2026, and without giving effect to any other changes in the inputs to the Exchange Ratio occurring after March 31, 2026, former REFI stockholders would be expected to own approximately 50.5% of the Company immediately following the Merger. The actual pro forma ownership percentage will depend on the Exchange Ratio calculated shortly prior to closing and may differ from the March 31, 2026 estimate.

 

On July 31, 2026, the Company filed a preliminary registration statement on Form N-14, as amended via Pre-Effective Amendment No.1 on September 23, 2026, which included a joint proxy statement of the Company and REFI and the Company’s prospectus (the “Proxy Statement/Prospectus”). The Proxy Statement/Prospectus is subject to review by the SEC. Once the Proxy Statement/Prospectus is declared effective, the Company will file its final joint proxy statement/prospectus with the SEC and begin mailing proxies to stockholders. The transaction is subject to approval by REFI and the Company’s stockholders and other customary closing conditions. Assuming these conditions are satisfied, the transaction is expected to close in 2027. There can be no assurance that the Merger will be completed on the anticipated terms or timing, or at all. Additional information regarding the Merger is set forth below under “Additional Information and Where to Find It.”

About Chicago Atlantic BDC, Inc.

 

The Company is a specialty finance company that has elected to be regulated as a business development company under the 1940 Act, and has elected to be treated as a regulated investment company for U.S. federal income tax purposes. The Company’s investment objective is to maximize risk-adjusted returns on equity for its stockholders by investing primarily in direct loans to privately held middle-market companies, with a primary focus on cannabis companies. The Company is managed by Chicago Atlantic BDC Advisers, LLC (the “Adviser”), an investment manager focused on the cannabis industry and other niche or underfollowed sectors. For more information, please visit chicagoatlanticbdc.com.
