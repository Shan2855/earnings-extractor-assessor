TransAct Technologies Reports Preliminary Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

 

Third Quarter 2026 Net Sales up 10% Year-over-Year

 

FST Revenue up 33% Year-over-Year; FST Software Revenue up 61%

 

Revises 2026 Revenue Guidance to $55 to $56 Million, Revises 2026 Adjusted EBITDA Guidance* to $1.5 Million to $1.75 Million

 

Hamden, CT – November 12, 2026 – TransAct Technologies Incorporated (Nasdaq: TACT) (“TransAct” or the “Company”), a leading provider of SaaS software and integrated hardware solutions, today reported preliminary results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

“Our food service technology business drove a strong third quarter, with FST revenue up 33% year-over-year with growth across hardware, software and labels,” said John Dillon, Chief Executive Officer of TransAct. “We sold 2,346 BOHA! units in the quarter, and software revenue grew approximately 61% as pricing actions, new account wins and bundled software packages take hold across our installed base. We are building a high-margin, software-led recurring revenue business on a growing installed base, and we are executing that transition with disciplined capital allocation and the strength of a solid balance sheet.”

 

“Casino and Gaming softened in the quarter, consistent with the cyclicality that we have seen in the past, and we expect the softer demand environment to continue into the fourth quarter. However, the fundamentals of this business are unchanged. It is a regulated market with high barriers to entry and a long-standing OEM customer base, and it remains a strong cash flow generator for the overall business.”

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

·Net Sales: Net sales for the third quarter of 2026 were $14.4 million, up 10% compared to $13.2 million for the third quarter of 2025 and up approximately 4% compared to $13.9 million for the second quarter of 2026. Food service technology (“FST”) net sales were $6.4 million, up 33% compared to $4.8 million for the third quarter of 2025, and Casino and Gaming sales were $6.4 million, down 11% compared to $7.1 million for the third quarter of 2025, reflecting the cyclicality of the business.

 

·FST Recurring Revenue: FST recurring revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $4.0 million, representing an increase of 24% compared to $3.3 million for the third quarter of 2025. FST recurring revenue includes software, labels and other recurring sources of revenue. More specifically, software revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $876 thousand, representing an increase of approximately 61% compared to $544 thousand in the third quarter of 2025.

 

·FST Online BOHA! Units – Online BOHA! units increased to approximately 22,600 units as of September 30, 2026, as compared with approximately 17,900 units as of September 30, 2025, representing 26% year-over-year growth in online units, net of retirements. Selling software, labels and other recurring sources of revenue into this growing installed base, is a key focus of management.

 

·Gross Profit: Gross profit for the third quarter of 2026 was $6.7 million, resulting in gross margin of 46.4%, compared to gross profit of $6.6 million for the third quarter of 2025, which delivered a 49.8% gross margin.

 

   
 

 

·Operating Income: Operating loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $(415) thousand, or (2.9)% of net sales, compared to operating income of $14 thousand, or 0.1% of net sales, for the third quarter of 2025 and an operating loss of $(54) thousand for the second quarter of 2026.

 

·Net Loss: Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $(440) thousand, or $(0.04) per diluted share, based on 10.3 million weighted average diluted shares outstanding. This compares to net income of $15 thousand, or $0.00 per diluted share, based on 10.2 million weighted average diluted shares outstanding, for the third quarter of 2025, and a net loss of $(50) thousand, or $0.00 per diluted share, based on 10.3 million weighted average diluted shares outstanding, for the second quarter of 2026.

 

·EBITDA: EBITDA was $(172) thousand for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $142 thousand for the third quarter of 2025 and $59 thousand for the second quarter of 2026.

 

·Adjusted EBITDA: Adjusted EBITDA was $289 thousand for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $669 thousand for the third quarter of 2025 and $514 thousand for the second quarter of 2026. Adjusted EBITDA for the first nine months of 2026 was approximately $2.2 million.

 

Update on Strategic Review

 

As previously announced, the Company’s Board of Directors (the “Board”) has initiated a formal strategic review of the Casino and Gaming business. Management engaged BofA Securities, Inc. (“BofA”) as its financial advisor. During the third quarter, management has been working closely alongside BofA as the Company continues to believe that exploring potential options within Casino and Gaming is in the best interests of stockholders as the Company looks to maximize stockholder value. While the review is focused on the Casino and Gaming business, the Board is also evaluating a broader range of strategic alternatives for the Company, including alternatives involving the FST business. These alternatives may include a potential sale of the Company’s FST business, whether separately or as part of a broader transaction, if the Board determines that such an alternative may further enhance stockholder value. The Company has not set a public timetable for the review, and there can be no assurance that the review will result in any transaction or other strategic outcome.

 

2026 Financial Outlook*

·Adjusted EBITDA: The Company now expects full year 2026 adjusted EBITDA to be between $1.5 million and $1.75 million.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

In addition to assessing the Company’s financial condition, results of operations, liquidity and cash flows in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”), the Company’s management team also utilizes certain non-GAAP financial measures, such as EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA (each as defined below), as part of its operational reviews to monitor, evaluate and forecast the Company’s financial performance, make operational and strategic decisions, communicate results to its Board and assess related employee compensation measures. These non-GAAP financial measures are compared to budgeted and forecasted amounts by the chief operating decision maker (CODM) on a regular basis to measure our progress towards our strategic plans, pursue product enhancements, and make any other necessary overall changes to the business. In addition, TransAct believes that these non-GAAP financial measures are helpful to investors in assessing the Company’s overall financial performance, trends and comparative results, including the ongoing nature of what the Company’s management views as TransAct’s core operations. The Company believes that these non-GAAP financial measures provide relevant and useful information to an investor evaluating the Company’s operating performance because these measures are: (i) widely used by investors to measure a company’s operating performance without regard to items that do not reflect the Company’s ongoing operations and are excluded from the calculation of such measures; (ii) used as financial measurements by lenders and other parties to evaluate creditworthiness; and (iii) used by the Company’s management for various purposes including strategic planning and forecasting and assessing financial performance. These non-GAAP measures exclude certain items, as defined below, including interest income, interest expense, depreciation and amortization expense and income tax expense, and, in the case of adjusted EBITDA, share-based compensation expense and shareholder advisory costs. The non-GAAP financial information contained herein is included for supplemental purposes only and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for or superior to the related U.S. GAAP financial measures. In addition, these non-GAAP financial measures are not necessarily the same or comparable to similar measures presented by other companies, since these measures may be calculated differently or exclude different items.

 

EBITDA is defined as net income (loss), as reported in the Condensed Consolidated Statements of Operations, before interest income, interest expense, income tax expense, and depreciation and amortization expense.

 

Adjusted EBITDA is defined as EBITDA (defined above) before (1) share-based compensation expense, (2) shareholder advisory costs associated with activism actions and (3) any other items, when they occur, that we believe do not reflect the ordinary earnings of the Company’s ongoing business. The Company excludes share-based compensation expense in this financial measure because the Company considers share-based compensation expense to be a non-cash expense similar to depreciation and amortization expense. The Company also excludes shareholder advisory costs related to third-party legal and advisory fees in connection with activism in this financial measure, as such costs relate to discrete matters, vary in timing and amount, are independent of the Company’s operating performance and do not represent the ongoing costs of the Company’s core operations. The Company began excluding these shareholder advisory costs in the third quarter of 2026; no such costs were incurred in prior periods presented.

 

The reconciliations of EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA to net (loss) income, their most directly comparable GAAP financial measure, can be found attached to this release.

 

   
 

 

About TransAct Technologies Incorporated

 

TransAct Technologies Incorporated is a leading provider of SaaS software and integrated hardware solutions that redefine how organizations connect operations, technology and data to drive measurable business value. Through its BOHA!® solutions, TransAct combines purpose-built hardware with a SaaS platform to help food service operators automate food safety, improve operational efficiency and maintain trusted brand relevance. In the casino and gaming market, TransAct’s award-winning EPIC solutions enable ticket-in/ticket-out (TITO) functionality and advanced promotional capabilities that enhance player engagement and drive revenue for operators globally. TransAct also provides a comprehensive portfolio of consumables and service solutions, allowing customers to simplify operations and partner with a single, trusted provider across their technology ecosystem.

 

TransAct is headquartered in Hamden, CT. For more information, please visit transact-tech.com or call (203) 859-6800.
